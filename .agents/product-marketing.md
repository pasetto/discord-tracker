## 1. Product Overview
- **One-liner:** Radar de colaboração no Discord — o gestor sabe quem está colaborando e quem sumiu.
- **Tagline:** Saiba quem está colaborando — e quem sumiu — no Discord do seu time.
- **Category:** Discord collaboration analytics / inactivity visibility (B2B SaaS)
- **Not:** legal timesheet, Jira, spyware, productivity surveillance
- **Model:** Multitenant SaaS, billing Stripe BRL, self-serve signup + onboarding (trial org sem cartão)

## 2. Target Audience
- Brazilian managers of remote/hybrid teams that already work on Discord
- Company size sweet spot: ~8–80 people on the work server
- JTBD (week 1): see who went dark with calendar/PTO context, without becoming a content police

## 3. Differentiation
- Passive Discord signals → inactivity alerts with work calendar + PTO context
- Metadata-only text signals (no message/audio/DM content)
- Channel scope configured in UI
- Live team view + individual collaboration goals
- Optional gamification (badges/streaks); ranking on Team+ plans
- Push alerts (day/week) + optional weekly email digest

## 4. Voice
- PT-BR B2B, direct, credible
- Use: colaboração, quem sumiu, metadados, gestor, alertas no dashboard / push
- Avoid: produtividade, spyware, ponto eletrônico, vigilância de conteúdo, “email/push” como paridade

## 5. Primary CTA
- **Criar conta** → app `/signup` (aprovado SYN-35; not waitlist)

## 6. Anti-claims
- No legal timesheet / certified LGPD without CEO validation
- No Jira replacement
- No reading messages / recording audio / DM spying
- No invented ROI % or fake testimonials
- No price table on v0 landing; teaser “Planos em BRL” only
